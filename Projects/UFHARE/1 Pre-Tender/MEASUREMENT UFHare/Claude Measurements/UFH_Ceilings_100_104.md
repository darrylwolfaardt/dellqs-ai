## 7. MEASUREMENT NOTES

### Assumptions
1. Ceiling heights from sections drawing A-(12)2000 - standard 2700mm AFFL
2. Room dimensions scaled from floor plan A-(11)1000 Rev 4
3. Bulkhead types interpreted from details on ceiling plan
4. Access panel locations estimated based on services layouts (HVAC, electrical)
5. Shadow line at all wall/ceiling junctions per typical detail

### Items Excluded
- Painting to ceilings (measured in Painting trade)
- Painting to bulkheads (measured in Painting trade)
- Services installations within ceiling void (Electrical, HVAC trades)
- Fire stopping at penetrations (Fire Protection trade)

### DQRules Applied
- Suspended ceilings: m² (Rule 5 - include bearers, battens, brandering)
- Cornices/shadow lines: m (Rule 57 - measured to mean length)
- Access panels: No (number)
- Bulkheads: m (linear) + m² (soffit area)
