## 5. SSM7 CLASSIFICATION

Per SSM7 Section 4 - Earthworks:

- **Filling:** Measured in m³
- **Classification:** By material type, average thickness
- **Compaction:** Deemed included unless specified otherwise (but see UFH-044)
- **Thickness stages:** Not exceeding 250mm (both layers qualify)

## 8. NOTES AND ASSUMPTIONS

1. **Material specification:** Hardcore assumed to be crushed stone or G5 material per structural engineer's specification.

2. **Compaction:**
   - Compaction of hardcore deemed included in filling item per SSM7
   - Separate compaction item (UFH-044) may be required if specific density testing specified

3. **DPM membrane:** Measured under waterproofing section, not earthworks.

4. **No deductions:** No deductions made for foundation/column positions (small areas).

5. **Bulking:** No bulking factor applied - volumes measured as compacted in place.

6. **Foundation trenches:** Filling to foundation trenches (backfill) measured separately if required.
