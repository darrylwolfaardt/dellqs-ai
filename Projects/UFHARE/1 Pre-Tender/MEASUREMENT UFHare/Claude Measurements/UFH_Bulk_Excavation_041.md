1. **Existing floor:** Assumed existing floor slab/construction to be demolished and removed (per demolition scope). New excavation required to formation level.

3. **Working space:** Not measured separately for surface beds (bulk excavation to full area).

5. **Compaction:** Compaction of formation measured separately under filling/surface bed items.
