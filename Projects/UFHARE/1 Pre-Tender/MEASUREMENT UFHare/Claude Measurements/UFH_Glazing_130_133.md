## 7. MEASUREMENT NOTES

### Assumptions
1. Window quantities estimated from fenestration key plan - verify on site
2. Shopfront sizes estimated from detail drawings - confirm with shop drawings
3. Glass thickness indicative - specialist to confirm per structural requirements
4. All dimensions to be verified by aluminium contractor before manufacture

### Items Included in Unit Rates
- Aluminium frames complete with finish
- Glass as specified
- All ironmongery (handles, stays, closers)
- Weatherstripping and gaskets
- Factory glazing and wrapping
- Delivery to site
- Installation in prepared openings
- Perimeter sealing

### Items Excluded (measured elsewhere)
- Structural openings (Masonry trade)
- Lintels over openings (Concrete trade)
- Painting to surrounds (Painting trade)
- Blinds/curtains (Provisional sum if required)

### DQRules Applied
- Windows: No (number) - include frame, glass, ironmongery
- Shopfronts: No (number) - include frame, glass, ironmongery
- Seals: m (linear)
