## 8. MEASUREMENT NOTES

### Assumptions
1. Sanitary fitting quantities based on WC areas and typical requirements
2. Pipework lengths estimated based on building layout
3. Hot water from single 150L geyser serving all outlets
4. Existing municipal sewer connection available
5. Water pressure adequate (no booster required)

### Items Excluded
- Water reticulation to site (municipal connection)
- Sewer connection fees
- Fire water supply (separate package)
- Rainwater goods (in Roofing)
