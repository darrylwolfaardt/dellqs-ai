## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

### 1.1 Project Description
Conversion of an existing old clinic building into Finance Department Offices at the University of Fort Hare Alice Campus.

### 1.2 Scope Summary
- **Building Type:** Office conversion (existing clinic)
- **Location:** Alice Campus, University of Fort Hare, Eastern Cape
- **Work Includes:**
  - Demolition of existing internal elements
  - New foundations and structural elements (ring beams, surface beds)
  - Internal walls and partitions
  - Complete finishes (walls, floors, ceilings)
  - Fenestration (doors, windows, shopfronts, gates)
  - Fixed furniture (security desk, kitchenette)
  - New external paving
  - Full MEP installations (Electrical, HVAC, Fire Protection)
  - Landscaping (R100k PC Sum)
